Buck's Eye

Buck's Eye is my first multi-part webcomic, still in the scripting phase. A folk horror story set on the fringes of a big city. Following a young girl labeled '20' as she escapes from a research facility while being haunted by a ghost. Will update twice weekly in the near future.
